#### Player and clan progress tracker.

D2 Checklist shows what you have left to do: raid and dungeon clears, weekly milestones,
triumphs, collectibles and PvP stats. It covers clans too, so you can compare progress
with your clanmates. The Destiny 2 version of the original Destiny Checklist.
